# Skill: ATS Keyword Optimization

Applicant Tracking Systems (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Taleo, iCIMS) rank resumes by how
well they match the job description before a human reads them. Your job is to maximise the
*honest* match.

## Rules
- **Mirror the JD's exact terminology.** If the JD says "CI/CD" use "CI/CD", not just "DevOps".
  Include common variants once (e.g. "Kubernetes (K8s)").
- **Place keywords where ATS weights them:** a Skills section, plus woven naturally into
  experience bullets (context beats a keyword dump).
- **Hard skills, tools, certifications, and methodologies** are the highest-signal keywords.
- **Do not keyword-stuff.** Repetition without context reads as spam to both ATS and recruiters.
- **Only claim keywords the candidate can support.** Suggest missing keywords as *gaps to close*,
  never silently insert skills they don't have.
- **Formatting that survives ATS:** standard section headings, no tables/columns/images for
  critical text, standard fonts. (Relevant when advising on resume structure.)

## When reporting
- Separate **matched** keywords (already present) from **missing** keywords (in JD, absent from
  resume). For each missing one, note whether it's a must-have or nice-to-have and how the
  candidate might legitimately add it.
