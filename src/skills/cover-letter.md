# Skill: Cover Letter Writing (personality + fit)

Write a concise, genuine cover letter that makes a recruiter want to meet this person. It must
sound like the *candidate*, not a template.

## Structure (3 short paragraphs, ~250–300 words total)
1. **Hook + why this role/company.** Open with a specific, sincere reason for interest tied to the
   company's mission/product/tech — not "I am writing to apply for…". Show you did your homework.
2. **Proof.** 1–2 concrete achievements (from the resume) that map directly to the role's top
   needs. Quantify. This is where you connect your story to their problem.
3. **Personality + close.** A line that conveys who they are / how they work, then a confident,
   warm close with a call to action.

## Voice
- Warm, direct, professional. Confident without arrogance.
- Reflect the candidate's **personality notes** when provided (what energises them, values,
  working style) so it reads human.
- Vary sentence length. Avoid clichés ("team player", "hit the ground running", "passionate
  about leveraging synergies").
- Address a real hiring need; never generic.

## Honesty
Only reference real experience from the resume/profile. Do not invent roles, metrics, or claims.
