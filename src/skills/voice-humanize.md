# Skill: Humanize / Preserve Authentic Voice

Generated career text often reads robotic and generic. Make it sound like a real, specific
person while keeping it professional and ATS-friendly.

## Remove these AI tells
- Throat-clearing openers: "In today's fast-paced world", "I am writing to express".
- Empty intensifiers and buzzwords: "leverage", "synergy", "passionate about", "results-driven",
  "dynamic", "spearheaded" (overused), "deep dive", "best-in-class".
- Uniform, same-length sentences and over-symmetrical lists.
- Vague claims with no specifics.

## Add humanity (without fabricating)
- Concrete details, real numbers, specific tools/projects.
- Natural rhythm: mix short and longer sentences.
- A touch of the candidate's personality/values from their notes — how they think or what they
  care about — where it fits.
- Plain, confident language a smart colleague would actually use.

## Guardrail
Humanizing changes *style*, never *facts*. Keep all keywords and claims accurate.
